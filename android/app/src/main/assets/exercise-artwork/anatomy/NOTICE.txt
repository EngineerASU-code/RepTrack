RepTrack anatomy assets

The male front and back SVG maps are copied from the minimal assets in
suryamolly/muscle_mapper (MIT License):
https://github.com/suryamolly/muscle_mapper/tree/main/lib/src/assets/minimal
Source files: male_front_muscle_anatomy.svg and male_back_muscle_anatomy.svg

and secondary exercise groups. Copyright (c) 2026 Surya Mouly. The full MIT
license is included beside these assets.
